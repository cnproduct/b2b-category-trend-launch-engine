- **Category Query**: `"toys"` (USA, past 7 days)
- **Breakout Keywords**:
  - `collectible bag charm toys` (+160%)
  - `promotional blind box toys` (+150%)
  - `kids meal toy set` (+130%)
- **Benchmark Source**: Global E-Commerce & Secondary Marketplace Data
- **Reverse-Engineered Specs**:
  - 15 collectible character figurines with dual-function desk stand & quick-release swivel clip
  - Packaging: 2 collectible foil trading cards bundled per blind mystery bag
  - Material: Food-Grade ABS + PP, ASTM F963-23, EN71-1/2/3, CPSIA certified
- **Commercial Offering**:
  - FOB $0.52 – $0.85 / pc (Tiered: 1K, 5K, 20K pcs); $8.50 / set for full 15-in-1 master box.
  - MOQ: 1,000 pcs; Express sample: 48 hours.
- **Sites Deployed**: Multi-site B2B toy portal cluster with Zero `.html` clean URLs.
- **GSC Submission**: Google Search Console sitemap ingestion & priority URL inspection completed.
